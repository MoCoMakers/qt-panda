Goals

G1 · Resolve real surface structure at ≤ 10 nm windows
Images whose features repeat after drift correction (≥ 0.7) and scale correctly with zoom.
G2 · Safe, repeatable automated scanning
AI-driven sweeps on request, with guards; no tip crashes from procedure.
G3 · Trustworthy numbers
Calibrated nm, a known meaning for every colour, clean signal chain.
G4 · Drift-locked imaging
Stage tracking that measures drift live and, when in-zone, holds the patch in place.

EXTRA: Gap calculator: bias, current and tip height

Tunnel-junction (gap) resistance R = |bias| ÷ current is set by the vacuum gap, not by the preamp's fixed 100 MΩ feedback resistor (which only sets the gain). Tip height above point contact from R = Rc·e2κs, with Rc ≈ 12.9 kΩ (one conductance quantum) and κ = 0.512·√φ Å⁻¹. Gold's own resistance is negligible. Heights are relative to point contact and depend on the barrier φ; an I(z) measurement would calibrate it. Valid at low bias (|V| ≪ φ).
Rule of thumb from the formula: the distance margin to the clip depends only on the ratio of clip current to setpoint, not on the bias; bias moves the whole working height. Each factor of 2 in current is a fixed height step.

Safety rules (apply to every session)

  1. Until the new firmware is flashed, the Retract (RTRC) button jumps Z to 50000, toward the sample whenever Z is below that (lower Z = away). Back off with the GUI Z slider or the motor. The fix (F2: Z to the fully retracted end) is built, not flashed.
  2. Choose the scan mode deliberately: with feedback off, RUN now asks (engage first, or constant height on purpose), and the clip guard steps Z away at sustained clipping (F1, built; needs a GUI relaunch to take effect). The Oct 22 crash happened parked at fixed Z.
  3. Approach and engage stay with the operator; Claude never approaches.
  4. Automated scans run with guards that halt (never move Z): clipping, Z limit, no-current share, feedback dropout.
  5. Analog supply on ~5 s before the Teensy, then Reset.
  6. Very low bias (below ~20 mV) brings the tip close; treat it as crash-risk.
